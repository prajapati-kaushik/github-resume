## Why it matters

Billing is revenue. Rules for VAT, reprints, access and recurrence belong in the domain, not in a PDF template.

## What I delivered

- Modular PHP structure for invoice, recurring billing, reminders and timesheets
- VAT/tax calculation on the invoice path
- PDF generation for bills and print/envelope formats
- Role-based access around billing workflows
- Backend and frontend as the product required

## For an organisation

I turn messy commercial rules into **modules a team can extend** — so the next country, reminder flow or tax change is a change, not a rewrite.
