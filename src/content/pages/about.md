I help organisations grow products that people already depend on. That usually means software that is already live: a patient portal, a billing product, a booking flow. My job is to keep it reliable, connect it to the rest of the business, and add the next capability without taking the product offline.

I am a senior engineer. The tools I use most often are PHP and Symfony. Recruiters and hiring managers should know that — but it is not the whole story. What I actually sell is this: I can sit with a live product, understand how the organisation uses it, and deliver work that makes the company safer and faster to change.

I have spent more than ten years doing that with a Dutch product company and international teams, remotely from India, on European hours. Before that I spent fourteen years at IndiaNIC, growing from developer to senior engineer on commercial web platforms. I like long relationships with products. I do not do well as a visitor who ships a feature and disappears.

## What I bring to an organisation

- **Continuity.** The product stays available while it improves.
- **Clarity.** I can explain the problem, the plan and the outcome to people who do not write code.
- **Ownership.** I pick up a piece of work and see it through.
- **Partnership.** Freelance or contract, but I work as part of the team — not as a ticket machine.

## How I work

- Freelance / contract, remote from India, overlapping European mornings and afternoons
- I prefer long product engagements over one-off builds

## Career

**LinkORB Engineering** (Dutch product company) — senior engineer, freelance / contract. Live products including [ZwangerenPortaal.nl](https://www.zwangerenportaal.nl/), used by millions of new parents in the Netherlands.

**IndiaNIC** (Ahmedabad, 2007–2021) — developer to senior engineer. End-to-end delivery on commercial web platforms: from understanding the need to a release the client could use.

**Intelsoftsolutions** (2006) — first professional role. That is where I learned that software only matters if someone can run a business on it.

## Tools (when they are useful)

PHP and Symfony for most of the products I look after. Also MySQL, and whatever the product already uses — older PHP frameworks, Docker, automated checks — when that is what keeps the organisation moving. I stay current, including with AI, when it helps me deliver a result a team can run.
